Account & Data Deletion Request — Daniel Quiz

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the same address you signed in with.
  2. Subject: Delete my account
  3. Send to: anas@formwear.com

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address and profile picture URL.
  • Your account identifier (UID).
  • All scores, times and levels.
  • All of your rows in every leaderboard.

Nothing is retained afterwards. Technical server logs are handled by Google under Google's own policy and contain no personal data of yours.

Timeframe

Requests are completed within 30 days and confirmed by email.

Playing as a guest?

Guest scores never leave your device — we hold no data about you. To erase them: Android Settings → Apps → Daniel Quiz → Storage → Clear data.
